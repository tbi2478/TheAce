WHAT WOULD ONE PROPOSE WOULD HAPPEN TO A NAÏVE HUMAN WHO THINKS SHE IS SUPERIOR TO THOSE WHO ARE NOT EVEN HUMAN. WHO ARE NOT EVEN FROM HER HOME. THE VERY THINGS, WHO ARE IN THE SAME PLACE BUT SEEK FEAR, GUILT, PAIN AND SHAME. TO THEM, DEATH IS NOTHING BUT DEVOURING DREAMS, POOR LIVES; SOMETHING THAT THE MONSTER WILL ACHIEVE FOR THE INHUMANE, UNETHICAL, DESCRIBED BY THE SAME HUMANS AS A GIFT TO ONESELF. HOW FAR WOULD YOU TO DESCRIBE YOUR NAÏVETY? Lily Manson fell into a deep rabbit hole after she discovers that a few of her university professors have been murdering and sacrificing young women, selling their souls in exchange to make a deal of cards with the devil, Polka. As Lily becomes entangled by the concept that everything is a perception. Set in 1972, 'The Ace' follows Lily's experiences, where she describes the effects of Akumu, a place where lost souls wander through what is often described as "The Hallways of Sadness and Trauma". How much can one truly understand about a world that was nothing but a twisted perception of reality? P.S. : No Artificial Intelligence was used during the production of the story.
